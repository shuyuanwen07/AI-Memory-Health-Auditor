formal independent evidence is incomplete. No empirical mark/accuracy is promised.

Additional user requirements (separate from proposal Table 2):
UX1 user-friendly presentation (required across all user-facing screens):
Use consistent English names and React controls, descriptive labels instead of
internal IDs, and clear loading, empty, pending, success and recovery states.
Charts must explain their dimensions, denominators, missing results and comparison
limits. Distinguish target improvement from a change in assessment standards.
Keep technical settings secondary; preserve original evidence and technical export
identifiers for verification. Check every screen through actual user operations;
this requirement is not complete until the full presentation audit passes.
Acceptance: a user can identify what was tested, understand the result and its
limits, inspect the supporting evidence, and find the next action without knowing
internal system terminology. Apply to every page, dialog, chart and feedback state.

Research validity display: ResearchValidityChart.tsx combines Precision/Recall/F1
across extraction, relationships, test validity and evaluator stages; explicit
missing values, stage counts, accessible values and expandable confusion counts.
F1 is calculated from raw counts and preserves real zero vs undefined denominator.
Backend research metric tests and frontend workflow/chart tests cover these cases.
Synthetic UI checks do not establish independent research validity.

UX2 concise visual results (mandatory presentation requirement):
Prefer charts for research outcomes, group dimensions and comparable conditions
into shared comparison views, and remove redundant prose/cards. Retain only a
short conclusion, essential chart labels/denominators/uncertainty/comparison limits,
and actionable next steps. Keep source answers, evidence and detailed methods
available on demand; visual simplification must not turn pending or untested
results into zero, obscure assessment changes, or imply unmatched comparisons.
Workflow counts are not model-health evidence and must remain labelled accordingly.

Visual acceptance checks:
- Comparable models, memory policies and audit methods share grouped charts.
- Before/after results show both values and their difference in the same view.
- Primary results avoid duplicate cards, charts and paragraphs for the same metric.
- Text remains for units, sample counts, pending states, limitations and actions;
  detailed methodology and original evidence are expandable.
- Desktop and narrow layouts keep labels and legends readable.
