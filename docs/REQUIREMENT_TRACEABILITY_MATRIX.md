## Status rule

Engineering implementation and local contract checks do not establish model quality or research validity. After the user's approval, a saved two-question Accuracy-only audit completed target execution and deterministic evaluation; it scored 0/2, and no human assessment established whether those automated failures were valid. The deterministic diagnosis endpoint returned cases, but the model-based diagnostic Agent was not run. An earlier run safely rejected generated questions after bounded repairs, and the saved smoke still lacks evidence for Freshness, Conflict Resolution, and Appropriate Use. A separate expanded synthetic candidate set has user-approved facts and cited relationships for engineering smoke only; it has no independent research inventory or audit results. Release UI/export reconciliation and hands-on novice acceptance remain open. “Proposal research validation complete” additionally requires genuine independent reviewers, adjudication and agreement records, frozen held-out evidence, formal repeated experiments, and an authorised compatible LongMemEval subset. Until those records exist, report the relevant requirements as **pending evidence**; do not fill the gap with fixtures or model-generated labels.
