# Selected experiment evidence (29 October 2026)

This folder contains the latest database snapshot, newly run exploratory audits, and selected evidence underlying the dashboard's A/B/C and AC1–AC3 results. The earlier A/B/C evidence package remains available in `research/evidence-20261027/`. These archives contain experiment data; the user has approved publication of the included conversations.

- `key-experiment-evidence-20261029.tar.gz`: selected experiment summaries, paired analyses, review returns, adjudications and cost evidence. Paths inside the archive are relative to the workspace.
- `new-audits-20261029.tar.gz`: five recent exploratory audits, their failed generation attempts and the 36-answer summary. See `NEW-AUDITS.md` for scope and limitations.
- `new-audits-review-20261029.tar.gz`: original blank review forms and materials for the 52 new answers.
- `new-audits-two-ai-reviews-20261029.tar.gz`: returned AI reviews, source-bound comparison, unresolved differences and the user's subsequent confirmation of individual human review. Original AI declarations are preserved; human reviewer independence and exposure were not specified.
- `audit-database-final.sql.gz`: database snapshot after the 52-answer exploratory audits, excluding authentication sessions and transient locks. The later AC2 feasibility attempts are preserved in their own ledger, not this snapshot.
- `ac2-prospective-feasibility-20261029.tar.gz`: frozen synthetic sources, source review, full product-flow ledger and blank review materials for the later AC2 feasibility study. Most attempts failed generation; it does not establish AC2 validity.
- `ac2-generation-coverage-diagnosis-20261029.json`: post-outcome offline examination of the same histories; it is development evidence, not an independent test.
- `ac1-ac2-development-20261029.tar.gz`: the AC1 two-stage AI review drafts and human review package, and AC2 v37/v39/v40 development retests, blind materials and model probes. No new human judgments are asserted.
- `ac2-independent-feasibility-20261029.tar.gz`: newly authored synthetic sources, frozen v40/v6 product runs, AI-only review opinions and blank human-review packets; coverage and limitations are in the included summary.
- `ac1-ac2-review-return/`: later submitted AC1 and AC2 reviews, the user's confirmation of individual human checking, the seven separate AC1 adjudications, and a reproducible summary. Human reviewer number and independence for this batch were not specified; original AI declarations are retained. The AC2 return covers the same questions but includes changed reviewer-1 labels.
- `ac2-final-review-return/`: the returned AI-assisted reviews of 48 facts, 24 relationships and 60 questions, their source-bound reconciliation, and the user's subsequent confirmation of individual human checking. Original files retain their AI declarations; the human reviewers' number, independence and exposure were not specified. Invalid and uncertain findings remain in the results.
- `ac2-partial-model-development-20261029.tar.gz`: model-based development probes on the previously observed 30 histories, including no-suite failures, request receipts, the source-bound 15-question blank review packet, and the sampling-parameter caveat. These are not independent validity results.
- `ac2-fact-slot-prototype-20261029.tar.gz`: isolated candidate slot planner, 12 regression checks, a three-history model development probe and blank review forms. No product API validation or independent validity claim is implied.
- `ac2-fact-slots-product-development-20261029.tar.gz`: same-source three-history product-flow development rerun, its ten pending questions, blank review packet and single-AI source review. After a timed-out request and a read-only check found no background work or target responses, a subsequent execution request returned HTTP 409 while questions were unreviewed. It is not independent validation.
- `ac2-four-dimension-new-source-20261029.tar.gz`: frozen protocol, eight related synthetic histories, 32 generated questions, two separate same-model AI review passes and the original blank human review materials. The later returned judgments and the user's itemwise human-check confirmation are preserved separately in `ac2-final-review-return/`; the number and independence of the human reviewers were not specified. This is a bounded feasibility study, not independent human gold.
- `ac2-nine-history-development-20261029.tar.gz`: frozen nine-history development run, source-bound verification, usage receipts and two AI-only reviews with blank human-review forms. The synthetic histories were selected after prior failed attempts; the reviews are not human labels.
